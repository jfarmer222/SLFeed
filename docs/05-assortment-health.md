## Registered exceptions

Three positions sit outside a default guideline on purpose. Each is recorded in
`assortment_data.py` so it reads as a decision somebody owns rather than drift.

**Thorne prices above the Best sofa band** ($2,299 vs $1,399–$1,999). Top-grain
leather is ~$330 of COGS on a sofa; it cannot be built inside a fabric-sofa
band at a Best-tier margin. Leather runs its own band, which is how the market
prices it.

**Thorne carries 6 covers, below the 12–25 Best range.** Leather is the product;
six well-chosen hides beat sixteen mediocre ones, and leather cannot be
reordered in small lots.

**The 2-Pc chaise sectional lane has no Best rung.** Best-tier sectional is a
modular program starting at 3-Pc — a two-piece modular defeats the point of
modularity. The Best customer wanting a chaise buys RAV-SC3-X at $2,699.
Revisit only if 2-Pc demand at Better proves out and the step to 3-Pc is
measurably blocking trade-up.

## Open items requiring a decision

**1. No $599 opening sofa.** The line opens at $699. A $599 3-seat sofa at
entry spec models at ~$357 landed against a ~$305 allowance — the price point
is not reachable at an in-band margin. Two clean options:

- *Recommended:* add an apartment-scale 2-seat sofa at $549–$599. Fills the
  price point with a product that carries its margin honestly. Would take the
  line to 66 SKUs, or 65 by retiring one Good-tier chair.
- Accept a sub-48% loss-leader margin on one declared traffic SKU.

**2. No motion sectional.** The line has 9 sectional SKUs and 13 motion SKUs,
and zero overlap. Reclining sectionals are a significant mid-market volume
category. Filling it properly needs 3–4 SKUs (2-Pc power reclining chaise
sectional, 3-Pc power, console variants) at Better and Best — which means
either expanding past 65 or cutting elsewhere. **This is the largest single
gap in the line** and it is a scope decision, not an oversight.

**3. No stationary chair-and-a-half or swivel accent.** Both are common
add-on-sale drivers. Lower priority than the motion sectional.

**4. Vendor costing.** Every `landed_cogs` figure is modeled from target
margin, not quoted. The architecture is proven; the costs are not. Replace
`margin_target` with quoted landed cost as RFQs return, then rerun both
scripts — the checks are built to be rerun, and Layer 5 will re-flag anything
that moves.
