# Recliner Category Strategy — Slumberland

A category strategy for recliners at Slumberland Furniture, and only
Slumberland. Built on the structure of the `category-page-brief` skill,
widened from one web page to the whole category: floor, web, and supply.

**Status:** Draft 1. Every external fact is cited in the Sources section.
Items marked **[C]** are inferred or internal and must be confirmed before
this goes to a decision-maker.

---

## The strategy in one paragraph

Slumberland sells recliners through two brands with two different jobs.
**La-Z-Boy** is the name the customer walks in asking for, and it carries the
Better and Best rungs. **SL private label** holds the opening price and the
entry lift chair, built domestically so tariffs do not hit it. The customer
Slumberland builds for is the one the housing market is producing now: the
homeowner who is **staying put**, getting older, and upgrading the room they
already own. The moat is **scale that La-Z-Boy's own stores and the online
sellers cannot copy in Slumberland's markets**: 120+ stores across 12 Midwest
states, regional quick-ship distribution, top-dealer standing with La-Z-Boy,
and a mattress business that already knows this customer.

## 1. Demand: who is shopping, and why now

*(Skill section: search intent and keywords.)*

### The housing picture

The housing market is not moving people. That changes who buys a recliner.

- Existing-home sales ran at a **3.98 million** annual rate in August 2026, a
  14-month low. [1]
- About **half of all mortgages (50.6%) carried rates of 4% or below** at the
  end of 2025, down from over 65% at the 2022 peak. Moving means giving that
  rate up. [2]
- NAR cut its 2026 home-sales forecast from +14% to **+4%**, and expects
  mortgage rates to stay in the **6.5%–6.7%** range. [3]
- Owners are spending on the home they have. Harvard's Joint Center for
  Housing Studies projects remodeling growth of **2.1% mid-2026, easing to
  1.6%** by year-end. Older homeowners already account for **nearly half** of
  home improvement spending, against a historical 30%. [4]
- The population is aging fast. Americans 65+ are projected to grow from
  **62.7 million (2025) to 71.6 million (2030)**. [5]

**What this means:** fewer movers furnishing a new house, more stayers
upgrading the room they spend the most time in. For a stayer, the recliner is
the best-value upgrade in the house: one piece, daily use, and a clear comfort
payoff.

### Four shoppers, in priority order

| Shopper | What they need | Lead brand | Size of bet |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The aging stayer** (60+) | Easy to get in and out of, fits their body, safe | La-Z-Boy lift and power; SL entry lift | Largest. Grows every year. |
| **The upgrading stayer** | Replace a worn chair with a clearly better one | La-Z-Boy power | Core volume |
| **The value replacer** | A good chair at a price they can say yes to today | SL opening price | Protects traffic |
| **The mover** (upside) | A whole room fast, often with a reclining sofa | Both, sold as a room | Small now. The unlock play. See section 9. |

### Shopper behavior that shapes the plan

- **71%** of shoppers prefer to buy large items like sofas in a store. [6]
  Recliners are bought by sitting in them. The 120+ store floor is the
  selling tool, not a cost.
- Shoppers who do go online cite **price (27%)** and **free delivery (25%)**
  as the top reasons. [6] Slumberland has to match on delivery and hold price
  credibility at the opening rung.
- **Baby Boomers and rural consumers** are the most resistant to paying a
  premium for eco-friendly products (Provoke Insights, n=1,500, ages 21–65,
  September 2025). [7] Read this as a price-sensitivity signal, not a green
  one: a big share of Slumberland's customer needs an honest opening price.
- La-Z-Boy's own stores grew written same-store sales **3%** last quarter on
  higher **design sales, conversion, and average ticket**. [8] The customer is
  still paying up when the store helps them choose.

### Web search terms (volumes not validated)

Head term: **recliners**. Local modifiers matter most, because this is a
sit-before-you-buy product: *recliners near me*, *recliners [city]*,
*La-Z-Boy dealer [city]*. Need-based long tail: *lift chairs*, *power
recliner with headrest*, *recliners for tall people*, *small recliners for
small spaces*, *wall-saver recliner*, *rocker recliner for nursery*. **[C]**
Validate volumes in Search Console before committing content.

## 3. Assortment architecture: two brands, clear jobs

*(Skill section: merchandising and default sort.)*

### The rule

Each brand gets a job. They never compete for the same customer at the same
price.

| Rung | Price band (planning) | Brand | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | $499–$599 | **SL** | Hold the price the value replacer sees online. Traffic. |
| Entry lift | [C] | **SL** | The first yes for the aging stayer. |
| Better manual | $699–$799 | **La-Z-Boy** | Rocker and wall-saver. The name brand at a reachable price. |
| Better power | ~$999 | **La-Z-Boy** | The volume heart of the category. |
| Best power and lift | $1,299–$2,599 | **La-Z-Boy** | Power headrest and lumbar, the full lift range. |

Price bands draw on this repo's line (`data/sku_master.csv`) and on the
observed La-Z-Boy dealer recliner range of $559–$2,599 recorded in
`docs/06-vendor-architecture.md`. **[C]** Replace with Slumberland's actual
cost and retail.

### Why this split works for Slumberland

- **Tariffs favor domestic.** Imported upholstered furniture carries a
  **25%** Section 232 tariff through 2026, rising to **30% on January 1,
  2027**. [9] La-Z-Boy says its "primarily U.S. based supply chain minimizes
  our tariff exposure." [8] SL has to be domestic too, or it loses its price
  edge in 2027.
- **A domestic partner exists at scale.** Franklin Corporation already sells
  on Slumberland's recliner page. [10] It builds recliners and lift chairs in
  Houston, Mississippi, with 1.2 million square feet and 1,100 employees. [11]
  **[C]** Confirm who makes SL today. If it is not domestic, Franklin is a
  candidate.
- **La-Z-Boy covers lift well at the top.** Its power lift range runs in
  three tiers (Bronze, Gold, Platinum), with battery backup, and capacities
  up to 500 lb on one model. [12] SL only needs to own the entry lift rung.

### The other brands on the page today

The recliner page currently shows La-Z-Boy, Flexsteel, Ashley and Franklin:
145 chairs from **$499.99 to $14,999**. The top of that range is massage
chairs. [10]

**Recommendation:** narrow to La-Z-Boy + SL over time. Every unit moved off
a third brand adds volume to the two brands Slumberland has leverage with:
it builds allocation and program terms with La-Z-Boy, and cost with the SL
maker. Keep a third brand only where sales data shows it wins a customer
neither La-Z-Boy nor SL can. **[C]** Test by sell-through, not opinion.

### Default sort, and out-of-stock handling

- **Default sort:** in stock at the shopper's nearest distribution center
  first, then by best-seller within that set. The current default is
  "Featured," with no stated rule. [10] Leading with what can ship fast
  shows the scale advantage on the first screen.
- **Pin** one SL opening chair, one La-Z-Boy power chair and one lift chair
  in the first row. Every shopper type sees their entry point without
  scrolling.
- **Move massage chairs** ($12,999+) into their own subcategory, so the price
  filter and the page's price perception reflect real recliners.
- **Out of stock:** never a dead end. Show the quick-ship alternative in the
  same style and price rung, and the special-order date for the chair they
  wanted.

## 4. How shoppers find the right chair

*(Skill section: filters and facets.)*

Today's filters are color, brand, collection, availability, price and
upholstery. [10] They sort by what a chair looks like. The shopper buys on
how it fits and what it does.

**Add, in this order:**

1. **Function:** Power · Manual · Lift · Rocker · Swivel · Wall-saver
2. **Fit:** Petite · Standard · Big & tall, with weight capacity shown on
   every card
3. **Features:** Power headrest · Power lumbar · Heat & massage · Battery
   backup · USB
4. **Delivery:** In stock near me · Quick ship · Special order
5. **Brand:** La-Z-Boy · SL

**Crawl rules:** let Google index single-facet pages with real search demand
(*lift chairs*, *power recliners*, *rocker recliners*, *wall-saver
recliners*). Keep multi-facet combinations (color + price + fabric) out of
the index so they do not create thin, duplicate pages.

**In the store:** the same logic becomes the floor layout. Zone by function
(lift, power, manual), not by vendor, so an aging stayer walks straight to
the lift chairs.

## 5. What the recliner connects to

*(Skill section: internal linking.)*

- **Mattresses and adjustable bases.** Slumberland began as a mattress and

  the sale both ways.
- **Reclining sofas and sectionals.** The mover and the upgrading stayer

## 7. Local search and technical notes

*(Skill section: SEO and technical notes.)*

Scale works in search too. A national online seller has one page per
product. Slumberland can have **one page per store**, each ranking for
*recliners near me* and *La-Z-Boy [city]*.

- Build a recliner section on each store page, with local stock, hours and
  store schema markup.
- **Title tag:** Recliners — Power, Lift & Rocker | La-Z-Boy & More | Slumberland
- **Meta description:** Shop La-Z-Boy and Slumberland recliners in power, lift and rocker styles. Try them in a store near you. Fast delivery on in-stock chairs.
- Use a single canonical URL for filtered views. Put product markup (price,
  availability, reviews) on every card.

## 9. The moat: why this works at Slumberland and not elsewhere

This is the core of the strategy. Each advantage below comes from scale that
a single store or an online seller cannot buy.

| Scale advantage | What it becomes in recliners | Why others can't copy it |
|---|---|---|
| **120+ stores, 12 Midwest states** [13] | Chair in front of the shopper, near them. 71% want to buy large items in a store. [6] | Online sellers have no floor. |
| **Regional quick-ship distribution** [14] | In-stock chairs delivered fast; the default sort shows it. | A single independent store cannot carry the stock. |
| **Top La-Z-Boy dealer** [13] | Allocation, program terms, first look at new chairs. | Smaller dealers lack the volume. **[C]** Quantify. |
| **Buying scale for SL** | Exclusive domestic frames, priced where online wins today. | Needs volume across 120+ stores. |
| **Mattress heritage** [13] | Sit + sleep attach sale for the aging stayer. | La-Z-Boy's own stores do not lead with mattresses. **[C]** |
| **Franchise owners in local markets** [14] | Local sellers, local reputation, local search pages. | National chains run from one playbook. |

**The honest risk: La-Z-Boy is both a partner and a competitor.** It
operates about 380 La-Z-Boy stores, 234 of them company-owned, and keeps
buying independent stores. It says it is "disproportionately growing our
Retail business." [8] Slumberland's defense is the two-brand design: SL
builds a customer relationship that does not depend on La-Z-Boy, and the
scale advantages above make Slumberland the better La-Z-Boy outlet in its
markets. **[C]** Map La-Z-Boy stores against Slumberland stores to find the
markets where they overlap.

## 10. The housing unlock: two scenarios, one trigger

Plan for stayers. Be ready for movers.

**Base case (now):** housing stays locked. Lead with the aging stayer and
the upgrading stayer. Weight the floor, the page and the ad budget toward
lift, power and fit.

**Unlock case:** sales pick up as more owners give up low rates. One report
already finds one in three sellers giving up a rate below 5%. [15] When that
happens, movers need whole rooms, fast.

**Proposed triggers** (Slumberland to set its own):

- Existing-home sales above a 4.5 million annual rate for two months in a
  row (vs. 3.98 million now). [1]
- 30-year mortgage rates below 6%.

**When a trigger hits:** move to room packages (reclining sofa + recliner),
push the motion sectional, and advertise to buyers in Slumberland's markets.
Because the stock is regional and ships fast, Slumberland can make this
switch faster than a special-order competitor.
