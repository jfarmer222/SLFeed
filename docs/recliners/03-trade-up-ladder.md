# The Trade-Up Ladder — Why Each Step Exists

`02-price-architecture.md` sets the rails. This file is what an RSA actually
says, rung by rung, and what the customer sees when they stand up and sit down
in the next chair.

**The test every rung has to pass:** a customer standing ten feet away, with no
tag and no salesperson, can tell why this chair costs more than the one beside
it. If they can't, the slot is decoration.

---

## The one-sentence version

> **$299 gets the conversation. $699 gets the margin. $2,999 makes $899 look
> sensible.**

---

## The mission of each tier

| Tier / price / mix | Mission | What enters here | Who is buying |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Good** · $349–$399 · 5 slots (12%) · +2 off-slot at $299 | **Price access.** Prove the advertised number is real. | Manual rocker · track-arm and chenille covers · La-Z-Boy's verified floor at $399 | Traffic off the ad · first-time buyer · price-comparison shopper |
| **Better** · $449–$1,099 · 33 slots (77%) | **The whole business.** Walk the customer from "cheap enough" to "worth it," $50 at a time. | Push-back hi-leg · wall-saver · swivel glider · **POWER** · power headrest · power lumbar · leather | New household · the core comfort buyer · family and pets · small-space · big and tall |
| **Best** · $1,299–$2,999 · 5 slots (12%) | **Proof.** 11% of slots returning 19% of sales — and the reference point that makes the middle legible. | Tech (storage, USB, cup holders) · refined leather · oversized leather · heat and massage · zero-gravity | Comfort-driven trade-up · wellness · aspiration · brand validation |
| **Lift** · $599–$2,799 · 9 slots | **Independence.** The fastest-growing thing in the category, merchandised as comfort, never as medical equipment. | 2-position → 3-position → power → infinite → heat and massage → heavy-duty → sleep positioning | Aging in place — and, very often, their adult child standing next to them |

## Transition points — why customers move up

Each row is a sentence an RSA can say out loud without apologising for the price.

| Step | Name | What the customer gets for the money |
|---|---|---|
| **$299 → $349** | Off the ad | From a promotional special buy to a chair with a proper rocker mechanism and a cover that doesn't feel like a rental. **$50.** |
| **$349 → $399** | The name | La-Z-Boy Collage. The brand they walked in saying, at the advertised price. This is the single highest-conversion step on the floor. |
| **$399 → $449** | Tailoring | Hawthorn — better arms, better seat, the same brand. The first rung that is purely about how it's built. |
| **$449 → $499** | **It doesn't look like a recliner** | Charlotte high-leg. Push-back, apartment-scale, reads as an accent chair. The answer to "I don't want a recliner in my living room." |
| **$499 → $549** | **It fits** | Rowan wall-saver — reclines *from* the wall. For anyone who has measured the room and isn't confident. |
| **$549 → $599** | **It swivels** | Kensington Swivel. 360° and a glide. Same frame as the $449 Kensington rocker, visibly a different chair. |
| **$599 → $649** | Scale | Brutus snuggler — a cuddler wide enough for two, or one person and a dog. La-Z-Boy doesn't make one. |
| **$649 → $699** | **POWER** ⭐ | Draycoll. The footrest moves at the touch of a button. **The most important step on the floor** — everything below exists to get the customer to this chair. |
| **$699 → $749** | Power depth | Yogi Power, and Astor's deep wide seat with a three-cushion pillow back. Choice within power. |
| **$749 → $799** | **The headrest moves** | Pinnacle Power Headrest. One more motor, one more visible adjustment. |
| **$799 → $849** | Built for me | Randell — oversized big-man seat, reinforced steel frame, engineered for taller users. No discount required to sell it. |
| **$849 → $899** | **Power lumbar** | Rowlett. The ergonomic story begins: the chair supports your back, not just your legs. |
| **$899 → $999** | **Leather** | Trouper Leather. Wipe-clean, and the first upgrade the customer feels in their hand before they read a tag. |
| **$999 → $1,099** | Better ceiling | Pinnacle Leather. The last chair before the question changes from *which* to *how much*. |
| **$1,099 → $1,299** | **TECH** | Denali — storage consoles in both arms, USB ports, cup holders, power headrest. Where charging finally arrives. |
| **$1,299 → $1,599** | **Leather grade** | Flexsteel Refined, power swivel glide with power headrest *and* lumbar. A different grade of hide, visible from across the room. |
| **$1,599 → $1,899** | Scale at Best | Roman Leather Oversize. Premium leather for the customer national brands usually forget. |
| **$1,899 → $2,299** | **Heat and massage** | Contour SoCozi. The chair does something *to* you. The only heat/massage recliner in the governed set. |
| **$2,299 → $2,999** | **Zero-gravity, and the point** | Everest Power Swivel Glider. The most expensive recliner we sell **is a swivel glider** — it doesn't look like a recliner at all. The whole under-35 argument, made in one chair at the top of the floor. |

## Where the ladder is thin — stated, not hidden

1. **La-Z-Boy carries no power below $799.** The anchor brand jumps from manual
   at ~$800 to Tri-Power at ~$1,200. The $699 power hinge is therefore **Ashley**,
   and $749–$949 power depth is **Ashley and Franklin**. If La-Z-Boy will not
   build a simple-power rung in the $700s, that gap is structural.

2. **La-Z-Boy has two verified swivel gliders. Franklin has eleven.** The
   silhouette that converts under-35 buyers is not the anchor brand's strength.

3. **Charging is a negotiation, not an assortment.** No La-Z-Boy recliner at any
   price has verified USB-C, wireless charging or a cup holder. Franklin sells
   wireless charging at $768.99. Holding tech above $1,100 protects the anchor's
   share of the volume corridor and costs us the tech story where the under-35
   customer actually transacts. See **C-017**.

4. **No boucle cover was verified on any governed brand.** The cover story for
   the under-35 buyer currently runs on performance fabric and chenille. Boucle
   has to come from a line sheet, not from the open web.
