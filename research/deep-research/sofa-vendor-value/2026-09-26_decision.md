# Sofa line vendor value: six swaps and three fixes

- **Question:** Are the vendors and SKUs in the 65-SKU line the best-value sources?
- **What winning looks like (set by the line owner):** 6–8 one-for-one swaps
  that improve selection, clarity or value.
- **Depth:** medium. 84 sources (39 primary, 36 industry, 9 discussion); 80
  fetched, 4 snippet-only. Index in `sources.csv`. Plan in `plan.md`.
- **Date:** 2026-09-26

## 1. The answer

The line is mostly well sourced. The August 2026 vendor work holds up, and
most vendors are stable or doing well. The value is in six slots that do too
little today. There are also three price or spec claims that the evidence does
not support.

## 4. Adversarial pass

**Four self-critique questions**

1. *What would make this wrong?* If power sectionals don't sell at this
   retailer, swaps 1 and 2 put more than $6,000 of floor ticket into pieces that
   sit. The demand case rests on what makers launch and show, not on sell-through.
2. *Which sources carry the most weight, and how good are they?* Trade-show
   coverage (06–09) is editorial and trend-driven. Prices come from single
   dealers [20, 21, 22, 37], and several are deep "sale" prices. The H.M.
   Richards finding rests on a 2015 article plus an inference from where the
   website's images are hosted.
3. *What would a buyer who disagrees say?* See below, per swap.
4. *What wasn't looked at?* Sell-through data (Circana, Furniture Today data;
   not available), wholesale costs (not public), and the retailer's own sales
   history. That history is the best test of all six swaps.

**Strongest case against each swap**

| # | Counter-argument | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kingsley 5-Pc is where the customer first meets modular before Best. Cutting it weakens the step to Ravenswood | Fair. Keep 5-Pc as a special-order setup if Best Home's units are modular. The power sectional is the bigger miss |
| 2 | Ravenswood 6-Pc is the highest ticket and carries the most margin dollars | A $3,699–$3,999 power sectional carries a similar ticket. The 6-Pc can still be ordered as a Ravenswood setup |
| 3 | The $579 accent chair is Better's cheapest add-on; a $769+ swivel raises that add-on price | True. If the add-on price matters most, swap the Harlow club chair ($749) instead and keep the accent chair |
| 4 | The line's own analysis says a $599 3-seat sofa can't make margin. A 2-seat may also cannibalize the $699 sofa | This is a 2-seat, not a 3-seat. It needs a quoted cost before commitment. Cannibalizing a $699 opener at the same margin rate is an acceptable trade for traffic |
| 5 | H.M. Richards has the best published Good-tier spec [53]. Rooms To Go ownership may mean scale and better pricing | Quality is not the issue. A competitor controlling supply, cost visibility and delivery priority is. If ownership is confirmed and terms are good, keeping H.M. Richards is defensible, **but say so openly** |
| 6 | The Quincy chair matches the Quincy sofa; a swivel breaks the set. Younger doesn't publish swivel prices | Fair. This is the weakest swap. Drop it if the Quick Ship swivel quote lands above $1,299 |

## 5. Context that affects every swap

- **Tariffs favor this line's mostly domestic vendors.** Section 232 puts 25% on
  imported upholstered seating from Oct 14, 2025. The step to 30% was delayed
  to Jan 1, 2027 [87, 88, 96]. La-Z-Boy makes about 90% of its North American
  upholstery in the US [81]. Swaps 1 and 2 should stay with US-made motion.
  Catnapper Rockport is US-made [22].
- **The category is flat.** Orders were flat and shipments down 1% in 2025
  [11], and 2026 looks similar [96]. New pieces have to take share.
- **Closures are still happening** (Kroehler Dec 2025, Prepac 2026) [84, 85].
  A listing is not proof a vendor is healthy.

## 6. What this does not replace

- Line sheets and quoted landed cost for every "in" piece. All target retails
  here are street-price benchmarks, not costs.
- Written confirmation of H.M. Richards' ownership.
- The retailer's own sell-through by piece type, which is the real test of
  swaps 1–4 and 6.
- Rerunning `ladder_health.py` and `spec_checker.py` after the swaps are
  entered, with a motion-sectional band added.
