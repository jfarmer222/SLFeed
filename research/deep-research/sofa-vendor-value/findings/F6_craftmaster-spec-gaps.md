# F6: Two Craftmaster slots are specced beyond what Craftmaster publishes

**Status:** supported on the gap. Not a reason to change vendor.

- Thorne (leather, Best): the line specs pocketed coil and 2.2 lb foam.
  Craftmaster publishes sinuous springs and 2.0 lb HR foam as standard [61]; its
  coil option is "Blend Down Coil", a coil-core cushion, not a coil deck [60, 64].
  Frame warranty lifetime, springs 5 yrs, cushions 10 yrs [62].
- Oakhurst sleeper (Better): the one Essentials queen sleeper found lists at
  $1,699.99 on sale [63] vs $1,499 in the line. No public source names the
  mechanism or mattress. No-bar mechanisms now exist near this price [71].
- Alternatives are not proven better: Natuzzi Editions Vara top-grain at
  $1,494–$1,660 is snippet-only [73]; Simon Li uses split leather on outsides [65];
  bar-free sleepers like American Leather and Luonto cost $3,299+ [69, 70].
