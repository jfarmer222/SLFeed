## Relevance
SUPPORTS the modular-sectional/apartment-adjacent demand thesis: Lovesac (the category leader in modular sectionals) is deliberately expanding beyond its core Sactionals into a new lower-priced, simplified sofa/loveseat/chair platform (EverCouch) explicitly to reach a broader market. But note fiscal 2025 net sales actually DECLINED 2.8% with comps down 9.3%, showing that even a modular-sectional specialist faced real top-line softness — a caution against assuming category momentum alone guarantees sales.
