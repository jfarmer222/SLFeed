## Relevance
OPPOSES a confidently bullish demand narrative: even before the October 2025 tariffs, the "big and bulky" furniture category (which includes sofas/sectionals) was described by a major retailer CEO as unusually unpredictable, with online sales roughly flat/declining — context that any new SKU line launches into a soft overall demand backdrop, not a rising tide.
