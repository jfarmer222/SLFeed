## Verbatim quotes
> Lifetime Warranty: "Blue Steel Springs, Wood Frames, Reclining Mechanisms, Seat Cushion Foam, Feathers" and "Drawer Glides"
> 5-Year Warranty: "Electrical Components, Mechanical Components, Sleeper Mechanisms, Sleeper Mattresses"
> 1-Year Warranty: "Finished Wood, Plastic Components, Metal Components, Battery Packs, Filling Materials, Upholstery Materials, Heat & Massage Components"
> Coverage applies to the original purchaser for products used under "normal, indoor residential conditions" and covers "manufacturing and material defects," remaining valid "for as long as the product remains in the original upholstery and for as long as parts are available."
> Exclusion: does not cover "wear and tear or damage resulting from neglect, abuse, misuse, rental or commercial use, pets, extreme temperatures, exposure to sunlight."

## Relevance
Confirms the prior-research finding: Flexsteel's electrical/motor warranty is 5 years, with reclining mechanism and frame covered under a lifetime warranty. This is the baseline against which La-Z-Boy, Southern Motion, Catnapper, and Franklin motor warranties (all found to be 1–3 years, see sources 24–27) are compared — Flexsteel has a materially longer electrical/motor warranty than every other motion vendor researched.
