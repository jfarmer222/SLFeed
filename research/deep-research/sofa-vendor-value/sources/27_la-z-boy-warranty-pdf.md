## Relevance
This is a critical finding for the warranty comparison (question 3): La-Z-Boy's power recliner motor/electrical systems carry only a 3-year warranty ("Electrical Systems/Motors ... 3 Years"), not the "limited lifetime" mechanism warranty that covers frame/mechanism/springs. This means on the specific electrical/motor component — the part most likely to fail in power motion furniture — Flexsteel's 5-year warranty is actually LONGER than La-Z-Boy's 3-year motor warranty, contradicting any assumption that La-Z-Boy's warranty is uniformly superior. La-Z-Boy's "limited lifetime" applies to mechanism/frame/springs only, same general structure as Flexsteel, Southern Motion, Catnapper and Franklin (lifetime mechanism, short motor warranty) — La-Z-Boy is not an outlier in this respect except that Flexsteel's motor coverage (5 yrs) beats La-Z-Boy's (3 yrs).
