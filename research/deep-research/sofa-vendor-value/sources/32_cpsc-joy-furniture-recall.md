## Verbatim quotes
> "The power switch on the Talan sofa and recliner and the Royce sofa, loveseat, and recliner can malfunction and overheat posing a risk of serious injury from a fire hazard."
> Approximately 8,200 units affected: Talan Power Sofa (JF1126-4PHL), Talan Power Recliner (JF1126-9PHL), Royce Power Sofa (JF1155-4PHL), Royce Love Sofa (JF1155-5PHL), Royce Recliner (JF1155-9PHL)
> Talan sets sold January 2024–March 2026 for $1,000–$2,200; Royce sets sold May 2025–mid-May 2026 for $800–$1,800
> "The firm reported 41 incidents including smoking, burning, and electrical odors, with two resulting in fires. No injuries were reported."
> Remedy: free replacement of the power recliner switch with an upgraded model, installed by an authorized technician at the consumer's residence.

## Relevance
Directly responsive to question 5 (CPSC recall check on power recliners): confirms that power-switch/electrical overheating is a real, current (2026) industry-wide failure mode in entry/mid-priced power motion furniture, not unique to any one of the vendors under evaluation (Joy Furniture is not one of the five core vendors named in this line, but the hazard class — power switch overheating causing fire — is the generic electrical risk relevant to any Better/Best-tier power SKU decision, and should inform vendor electrical-component quality diligence).
