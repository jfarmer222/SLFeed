## Verbatim quotes
> "The integrated cupholders with LED lighting in these power recliners can overheat, creating a fire danger."
> Approximately 253,000 units in the U.S. and ~9,450 in Canada. Models: Loveseat 3700318/3700418/3700318C/3700418C; Sofa 3700315/3700415/3700315C/3700415C; Recliner 3700313/3700413/3700313C/3700413C
> "six reports of the cupholder with LED lighting overheating, resulting in fire and smoke damage and damaged furniture." No injuries reported.
> Sold November 2018 through March 2023, priced $900–$1,800.
> Remedy: free repair (unplug immediately, contact Ashley Furniture Industries).

## Relevance
Ashley (the line's current Good-tier vendor for manual recliners) has its own documented CPSC recall history on power recliner/sofa products (Party Time line), showing that even the incumbent Good-tier vendor is not free of electrical/fire-hazard recall history when it makes power motion furniture — useful context for question 5 (recalls) even though Ashley's manual-only Good-tier SKUs in this line are not directly implicated; it flags a class-wide risk (cupholder/electrical component overheating) to check on any candidate vendor's power SKUs before adoption.
