## Relevance
This is the single most important construction-spec source for the "Thorne" comparison. Craftmaster's standard/base construction across its lines uses **sinuous wire springs**, not pocketed coil, and **2.0 lb HR foam** (not 2.2 lb as claimed in the "Thorne" spec sheet), with a hardwood-rail-plus-plywood-laminate frame (not solid kiln-dried hardwood throughout). Mortise-and-tenon joinery does match the "Thorne" claim. This means the proposed Thorne spec (pocketed coil, 2.2 lb foam) appears to describe an upgraded/custom configuration beyond Craftmaster's stated baseline — a claim that needs verification with the actual dealer order sheet before being presented as standard.
