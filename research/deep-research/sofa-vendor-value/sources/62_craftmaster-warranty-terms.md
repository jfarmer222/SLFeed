## Verbatim quotes
> "protection against manufacturing defects in the frame" for "the lifetime of the product, provided it remains upholstered in the original fabric or leather and is used under normal conditions"
> Springs covered "for a period of 5 years from the date of original purchase against breakage and manufacturing defects under normal use and conditions"
> Seat cushions protected "against abnormal loss of resiliency" for "10 years from the date of original purchase"
> Leather "warranted against defects in leather material or workmanship for a period of one year from the original date of purchase under normal use and care"
> Fabric "covered against defects in fabric material or workmanship for a period of one year from the original date of purchase"

## Relevance
Establishes Craftmaster's actual warranty structure: lifetime frame / 5-yr springs / 10-yr cushions / **only 1-year on leather itself**. This is materially shorter than warranties offered by higher-end American leather makers (Omnia: 5-yr leather; Bassett positions itself similarly), and is a real weakness to weigh against Craftmaster's price advantage when deciding whether it is the "best value" at the Best-tier price point.
