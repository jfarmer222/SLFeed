## Relevance
Simon Li is a lower-cost Shanghai-manufactured leather sofa brand often sold near the $1,700–$2,000 range (via Costco and Wayfair/AllModern), directly competing with the proposed Thorne price band. This review finds Simon Li uses plywood frames (vs. Craftmaster's hardwood-rail/plywood-laminate hybrid) and only top-grain leather on primary seating surfaces with split-leather match elsewhere — a lower spec than the "6 hides, top-grain" claim for Thorne. Useful as a lower-tier comparison point showing Craftmaster/Thorne likely out-specs Simon Li at a similar price, but not as strong opposition to Craftmaster.
