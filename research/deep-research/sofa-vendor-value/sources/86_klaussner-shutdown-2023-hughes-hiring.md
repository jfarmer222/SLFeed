## Relevance
Klaussner (Randolph/Montgomery County, NC — not one of the retailer's 13 vendors) collapsed in 2023 when Bank of America withdrew financing, laying off ~900 workers; its receivership formally closed July 2025 with the brand sold to Jennifer Furniture. Directly relevant to Hughes Furniture Industries (Randleman, NC, one of the retailer's 13 vendors), which multiple 2025 search results describe as "hiring displaced Klaussner employees" — i.e., Hughes appears to be a net beneficiary of a competitor's earlier collapse, which is opposition/strength evidence for Hughes even though the underlying Klaussner failure is older news reaching a formal conclusion in the 2025 window.
