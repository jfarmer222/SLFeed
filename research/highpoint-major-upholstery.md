# High Point Market — Major Stationary Upholstery Resources

**Prepared for:** mid-market furniture retailer (~125 stores), building a 65-SKU stationary upholstery assortment
**Target retail band:** $699–$2,500 sofas
**Research date:** 11 August 2026
**Scope:** stationary sofas, loveseats, chairs, sectionals, ottomans. Motion/recliner specialists are covered by a separate workstream and appear here only where the company is also a major stationary resource.

**Authoritative showroom source:** the official High Point Market exhibitor directory at `highpointmarket.org` (exhibitor detail pages, pattern `/exhibitor/{id}`). Every building + space number below was read directly from that exhibitor's own directory page and the page ID is cited in each profile.

**Method note on the directory:** the Market's category filter `Upholstered Furniture > Sofas / Loveseats` returns **450 exhibitors**. The companies profiled below were selected from that full enumerated list by scale and mid-market relevance, not by keyword guessing. The full 450-name list was reviewed; the secondary reference table at the end captures the significant names that did not warrant a full profile.

- **HQ:** New Tazewell, TN. **Parent:** La-Z-Boy Incorporated (acquired 1995). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_Furniture_Incorporated)

## 5. Shortlist recommendation

For a 125-store mid-market chain building 65 stationary SKUs at $699–$2,500, the strongest resources in order:

1. **England Furniture** — the 21-day custom quick-ship program is the single best answer to floor-sample-to-delivery risk in this band, and it publishes real construction specs (sinuous wire, reversible ventilated block foam) with dealer-visible starting prices. Domestic, La-Z-Boy-backed.
2. **Jackson Furniture (Jackson label)** — 10 plants, 1,500+ people, clean stationary/motion brand split, and by far the best free public spec data (piece-by-piece frame tables, named suspension and framing systems). Easy to build a sectional program from.
3. **Craftmaster (incl. Craftmaster Essentials)** — deep cover library (800+ core, 100+ performance on Essentials), full published dimensional specs, real cushion-construction detail (Blend-Down, 2.0 HD/HR foam), lifetime frame warranty. Essentials is the tier that lands in band.
4. **Best Home Furnishings** — 1.1M sq ft domestic, 700+ fabrics, complete published dimensions. Verify current frames — its S-series rotates fast.
5. **Hughes Furniture** and **H.M. Richards** — the two deepest opening-price domestic stationary resources. Hughes builds its own frames; H.M. Richards cuts its own frames *and* its own foam. Both are how you hold a $699 opening price with domestic content.
6. **Flexsteel** — the Blue Steel Spring is a defensible, differentiated construction story for a step-up feature ladder, backed by public per-model specs and a $441.1M public company.
7. **MAX Home** and **Albany Industries** — 700+ and 450+ employee domestic MS makers respectively; MAX Home's Room & Board relationship is a useful quality proxy.

**Add with conditions:** **Fusion Furniture** is an excellent pure-stationary domestic maker but is eight months into Man Wah ownership — confirm program terms first. **Jonathan Louis** has the best mid-market sectional aesthetic but 6–10 week lead times and almost no public spec data. **Bassett** and **Rowe** sit at or above the top of the band and both have channel-conflict considerations (Bassett's own stores; Rowe's designer orientation).

**Biggest verification gap:** **showroom square footage is essentially unpublished across the category.** Of 18 profiled companies, zero have a verifiable published showroom size; the only usable figures found anywhere at Market were Bernhardt's 95,000 sq ft and an unverified ~150,000 sq ft for Ashley's IHFC H900. Building and space numbers are solid; size is not obtainable from public sources.
