**Prepared for:** Slumberland Furniture (~125 stores, Midwest value/mid-market), building a 65-SKU recliner gallery

**Scope.** This is the motion/recliner/lift workstream that `research/highpoint-major-upholstery.md` explicitly defers: *"Motion/recliner specialists are covered by a separate workstream and appear here only where the company is also a major stationary resource."* Companies already profiled in the stationary file appear here only where their motion or reclining business is the relevant part.

**Authoritative showroom source:** the official High Point Market exhibitor directory at `highpointmarket.org`. Every building and space number below was read directly from that exhibitor's own directory page (`/exhibitor/{id}`) and the page ID is cited in each row.

Every price quoted is a **public dealer or consumer price**, directional only, never a wholesale or program price.

## 2. Trend & innovation — what is actually new on the floor

Organized by theme rather than by company, because the point is what it means for our ladder, not who showed it. Sources: April 2026 High Point Market coverage and exhibitor pages.

### 2.1 Zero-gravity is moving from premium down into the middle
Ashley showed a sectional with **"P2 zero gravity"** positioning alongside immersive sound. Zero-gravity has spent a decade as a lift-chair and $3,000-motion feature; it is now appearing on mainstream national-brand goods. ([Home News Now, May 2026](https://homenewsnow.com/blog/2026/05/01/product-highlights-from-april-high-point-market-2/))

**What it means for us:** zero-gravity is the right *top* feature reveal on both our recliner and lift ladders today, but it will not hold that position for more than a season or two. Do not architect the $2,999 rung around it alone.

### 2.2 Wireless charging has moved from the chair to the console — with magnets
The notable charging execution was a console with a **wireless charging tray plus a pop-up magnetic charger** sized for smart watches and wireless headphone cases, not just phones. ([Home News Now](https://homenewsnow.com/blog/2026/05/01/product-highlights-from-april-high-point-market-2/))

**What it means for us:** this is the single sharpest reading of the under-35 customer on the floor — they are not charging one device, they are charging three. Our category file already lists *"adding storage, wireless charging, cupholder to new cover"* as a 2026 product change. **Franklin is the only governed brand where wireless charging was verified at retail** (Gradin, $768.99), and La-Z-Boy showed none at any price. Because tech reveals are held above $1,100 in this build, this is a capability to negotiate into the anchor brand rather than merchandise around — logged as conflict C-017.

### 2.3 Immersive audio with seat vibration is the new ceiling feature
Nice Link's **Echolux** reclining sectional integrates **directional near-field speakers into the back and side arms, a deep-bass subwoofer, and audio-synced seat vibration**, at $4,999–$5,999 for six pieces. ([Home News Now, April 2026](https://homenewsnow.com/blog/2026/04/25/20-trending-products-to-see-at-high-point-market/))

**What it means for us:** above our $2,999 ceiling and sectional-shaped, so not a buy. But it re-prices what "loaded" means at the top of a motion floor, and it will pull heat/massage down-market as it does so.

### 2.4 Heat and massage are integrating at the group level
Flexsteel's **Brio** group carries **integrated heat and massage with Cozzia technology** in Italian leather. ([Home News Now](https://homenewsnow.com/blog/2026/04/25/20-trending-products-to-see-at-high-point-market/)) At retail, our own research verified La-Z-Boy **SoCozi at $1,999.99** as the only heat/massage recliner in the governed set, plus Franklin's **Apex lift with heat + massage at $976.99**.

**What it means for us:** Franklin is delivering heat + massage in a lift chair at **half** what La-Z-Boy charges for it in a recliner. That is the clearest value-per-feature gap in the whole study.

### 2.5 Scale is being merchandised as a choice — the most useful idea on the floor
Best Home Furnishings relaunched its **Beast** recliner line with new signage and two added styles (six total), and — the important part — ladders the **same chair in an extra-wide version at $1,200 and a smaller version at $999**. ([Home News Now](https://homenewsnow.com/blog/2026/05/01/product-highlights-from-april-high-point-market-2/))

**What it means for us:** this is the cleanest answer anyone showed to **Job 4 ("will it fit?")** and **Job 7 ("built for someone my size")** simultaneously. It converts scale from a spec buried on a tag into a signed, shoppable decision, and it does it without adding a style to the floor. Whether or not Best becomes a resource, **the merchandising idea should be stolen outright**: pick two or three high-velocity frames and floor them in two scales with scale-led signage.

### 2.6 Domestic + in-stock is being sold as a feature
Best ("weeks, not months," 1.1M sq ft in Indiana), HomeStretch ("maintained in stock for rapid delivery"), Franklin, Mega Motion and UltraComfort are all domestic and all lead with availability.

**What it means for us:** directly relevant to the category's open operational gap — **in-stock 85% against a 95% goal**. The 13 off-slot rapid-replenishment SKUs are the natural place to use a domestic in-stock resource, and availability is a feature the RSA can sell, not just a metric the buyer manages.

## 5. Data quality and caveats

1. **Showroom placements are directory-sourced and current as of 12 Sept 2026.** Space assignments change between Markets. Re-verify before booking a walk for the 17–21 October Market.
2. **No square footage is published** by the directory for any company here. Every entry is `NOT PUBLISHED` — none was estimated.
3. **Directory listing is not proof of solvency.** Southern Motion is the live example in this very file. Status was verified independently against trade press for every company.
4. **Prices in this file are public dealer or consumer prices**, drawn from trade coverage, and are directional only. They are not wholesale, not program pricing, and not comparable to our landed cost.
5. **Product categories are as the exhibitor describes itself.** Precision Reclining Chair Co.'s construction claims (memory-foam seats, coil spring, hardwood frames) are the company's own copy, not an independent teardown.
6. **La-Z-Boy was not scouted.** As the 64%-of-slots anchor bought through its own channel, it sits outside this file's purpose. Its absence here is deliberate, not a gap in coverage.
7. **Precedence.** Everything in this file is **Evidence (rank 6)** under `00-system/precedence.md`. It informs the assortment; it does not override `30-categories/recliners.md` vendor roles, the La-Z-Boy 64% commitment, or the `brand-roles.md` attribute contracts.
