# Niche & Design-Led Upholstery Resources at High Point Market

**Purpose:** Candidate specialist upholstery vendors to anchor the top of a 65-SKU assortment for a ~125-store mid-market retailer, alongside a national motion brand.
**Target band:** ~$1,500–$4,000 retail sofa, with a few reaching down to ~$1,200.
**Research date:** 2026-08-11. Next Market: **Fall High Point Market, October 17–21, 2026** (per exhibitor pages on highpointmarket.org).
**Method:** Public web only — official High Point Market exhibitor directory, manufacturer sites, public dealer/e-commerce pages, and trade press. No logins, no dealer portals.

> **Read the caveats before acting.** Showroom locations come from the official exhibitor directory, which is authoritative for *placement* but is **not** proof a company is still trading — see Leathercraft by OHD below, which is still listed and has been closed since August 2025. Every price shown is a **public dealer/consumer price**, not a wholesale or program price, and is a directional indicator only.

## 3. CR Laine Furniture Company

- **HQ / founded / ownership:** Hickory, NC. Founded **1958** by Clarence Roseman; **family-owned, third generation** (CEO C.E. "Sonny" Roseman Jr.; President Warren Frye). ~200 employees. ([CR Laine About](https://www.crlaine.com/about-the-company))
- **Showroom:** **310 N. Hamilton St. – S-204, Floor 2** (Hamilton Court). Shuttle Stop 18 (Red Line), Hamilton Wrenn. Sq ft NOT PUBLISHED. ([HPMKT exhibitor 704](https://www.highpointmarket.org/exhibitor/704))
- **Distinctive, concretely — the strongest published construction story in the group:**
  - **8-way hand-tied double-cone steel coil springs**, tied at eight points with no-squeak twine, over a platform of poly webbing and steel bands.
  - Frames of **thicker kiln-dried hardwoods and engineered hardwoods**, joints **glued, twice-doweled, and corner-blocked**.
  - Seat cushions: **high-resiliency soy-based foam** wrapped in fiber and encased in fabric ticking to reduce abrasion. Back cushions **baffled**.
  - Upholstery **hand-cut, hand-sewn, hand-tailored**, patterns aligned top-to-bottom and side-to-side.
  - Member, **Appalachian Hardwoods Manufacturing Initiative** (local, sustainably harvested wood).
- **Warranty (per dealer):** **Lifetime** on frame and suspension; **5 years** cushions; **3 years** motion mechanisms.
- **Lead time:** **~14–16 weeks** per dealer listings — the slowest in this set. This is the main strike against CR Laine for a 125-store floor program.
- **Cover program:** **75+ fabrics** on a given frame per dealer configurators, standard and performance grades; wood finishes (Teddy Bear Brown, Walnut, Java, etc.) and optional nailhead in multiple metals; bench-seat or standard-cushion option. COM policy: **NOT PUBLISHED**.
- **Product data public:** Yes via brand site and dealer configurators (crlaine.com intermittently returns 503 to automated fetches).

Shuttle Stop 0 (Go Anywhere Shuttle); neighborhood listed as "Other" — i.e., **off the main shuttle grid**, requiring a dedicated trip. Co-shows Asia Minor Carpets and Alkemis Paint. ## Data quality

### What is solid
- **Showroom building and space numbers** for 13 of 15 companies come directly from the official High Point Market exhibitor directory and are quoted verbatim. These are the highest-confidence facts in this document.
- **Construction detail** is well documented and primary-sourced for CR Laine, Temple, Wesley Hall, Cisco Home, Precedent, and Younger.
- **The Leathercraft/OHD closure** is confirmed by two independent trade-press articles with a specific date (8/31/2025).
- **Norwalk's ESOP**, **Cisco's HQ relocation to High Point**, **Verellen's 1999 founding**, and **CR Laine's ownership** are each confirmed by named sources.

### Known gaps and weaknesses — read these before quoting anything
1. **Square footage is essentially unavailable.** The exhibitor directory does not publish it. Only Cisco Home (60,000 sq ft, self-reported) has a figure. Everything else is **NOT PUBLISHED**, not estimated.
2. **Two showrooms lack a space number in the official directory**: Lee Industries (100 S. Lindsay St.) and Vanguard (301 N. Hamilton St.). Precedent gives a floor ("3rd Floor") but no suite. These are the directory's own limits, not research failures.
3. **Prices are dealer/consumer prices, never wholesale.** Several dealer sites (Hickory Park, Hickory Furniture Mart, Sofas Etc, LuxeDecor, Perigold, Goods, LA Waters, Paul Schatz, Jacksonville Furniture Mart) returned **403/429** to automated fetch, which blocked price verification for **Taylor King, Massoud, Precedent, and Temple**. Temple's ~$3,950–$4,935 figure appeared only in a search snippet and is marked **unverified**.
4. **Lead times are largely unpublished.** Only three are verified: Norwalk (**35-day target**), American Leather (**4–6 weeks**), CR Laine (**14–16 weeks**, dealer-stated). Younger's Quick Ship exists but publishes no day count. Everyone else: **NOT PUBLISHED**.
5. **COM/COL policy is nearly universally unpublished.** Massoud is the exception and publishes yardage/sq-ft per SKU. Do not assume COM is accepted anywhere else without asking.
6. **Two construction claims that circulate widely but I could NOT confirm on a company-controlled page:** Taylor King as 8-way hand-tied, and Omnia Leather as hand-tied spring. Both appear only in third-party retailer copy. **Ask at market.**
7. **Founding years with conflicts:** Omnia Leather (1989 vs. 1993 vs. 2003) and Wesley Hall (implied c. 1989 from a 2014 magazine reference; no primary source). Both flagged in-line.
8. **"Currently exhibits" is verified as: has a live exhibitor listing showing Fall Market Oct 17–21, 2026, plus corroborating trade coverage where available.** It is **not** a confirmed booth booking. The Leathercraft case proves listings outlive companies. Reconfirm every appointment directly with the vendor before building a market schedule.
9. **Model numbers were captured only where a manufacturer or dealer published them.** No model number, price, or spec in this document was inferred, reconstructed, or estimated. Where a value is unknown it says NOT PUBLISHED or NOT CAPTURED.
