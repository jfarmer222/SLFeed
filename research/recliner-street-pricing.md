# Recliner & Lift Chair — Market Street Pricing Research

**Researched / all prices pulled:** 2026-09-12 (single pull date; every row below was retrieved on this date)
**Purpose:** Source data for a 65-SKU recliner + lift-chair assortment for Slumberland Furniture (~125 stores, Midwest value/mid-market).
**Scope — governed vendor set only:** La-Z-Boy, Ashley (Signature Design / Benchcraft), Flexsteel, Franklin Corporation (FKLN). Lift-chair reference points from Golden Technologies, UltraComfort and Pride Mobility are included **as benchmarks only, not as proposed vendors.** Man Wah and Southern Motion are excluded entirely per instruction.
**This document does not design the assortment.** It is price intelligence only.

> **Read "Data quality" and "Price points we could NOT verify" before using any number here.** Two of the four named "special purchase $299" claims in the brief did **not** verify, and roughly a third of the specific model names supplied in the brief do not exist as recliners at all.

## Method & access summary

| Source | Fetchable? | What we got |

| **homemakers.com** (Homemakers, Urbandale IA) | Fetch **403**, but **search-result titles carry the price verbatim** (`Buy <product> for USD <price> \| Homemakers US`). Used as the **second street-price reference**. |
| **nfm.com** (Nebraska Furniture Mart) | Fetch **503**; a few prices recoverable via search snippets only. Thin. |

| **ashleyfurniture.com** | **No — HTTP 403** on every URL tried | Bot-blocked, not dealer-gated. A few Ashley list/sale prices recovered via search snippets quoting the site. |
| **furniturepick.com** | Fetch **403**; search snippets usable |
| **hom.com** | **Wrong domain.** `hom.com` is a French menswear brand. HOM Furniture is **homfurniture.com**. That site's category page renders navigation only, no products or prices. **No HOM Furniture prices verified.** |
| **wayfair.com** | Only keyword landing pages surfaced; no clean PDP prices captured. Not used as a price source. |

**Two-retailer triangulation.** Where a model appears at both Slumberland and Homemakers, both numbers are shown. They differ materially (Slumberland runs deeper off a higher "was"), which is itself a finding — see Data quality.

**Price labels used below:**
- **Street** = the price a consumer pays today on a live retail page.
- **"Was" / list** = the retailer's own strike-through comparison price. This is a **retailer-set reference price, not a manufacturer MSRP.** No vendor-published MSRP was obtainable for any of the four governed brands.
- **MSRP** column is populated only where a manufacturer or manufacturer-operated site published it.

**Mechanism decoding.** Where a PDP was not individually opened, mechanism is read off the product name, which is consistent and reliable across these retailers: `Rocker Recliner` = manual rocker · `Wall Recliner` / `Wall Hugging` = wall-saver · `High Leg Recliner` / `High Leg Reclining Chair` = push-back hi-leg · `Swivel Glider` / `Swivel Rocking` = swivel glider · `Power` = power recline · `Power Plus` / `Power Headrest` = power recline + power headrest · `Tri-Power` = power recline + headrest + lumbar · `Lift` = lift. Rows where mechanism is inferred from the name rather than read off a spec sheet are marked **(inf.)**. See Data quality for the one naming convention we could not fully confirm.

**Cover.** Only stated where verified. `NOT STATED` means the listing did not publish the cover and we did not guess. La-Z-Boy/Ashley/Franklin fabric models are overwhelmingly polyester or polyester-chenille, but we have not asserted that per-SKU without a source.

## Data quality

1. **Brand attribution on Slumberland's all-brand listing is unreliable; the brand-filtered collection pages are authoritative.** The combined `/collections/recliners` listing mis-assigned brands in several cases (it labeled Everest as Franklin when the Flexsteel collection lists it; Elsa as Ashley when the Franklin collection lists it; Dutton as Flexsteel when the La-Z-Boy collection lists it; Lavenhorne as La-Z-Boy when the Ashley collection lists it). **Every brand attribution in this document comes from the brand-filtered collection page, not the combined listing.** Any downstream agent re-pulling Slumberland should do the same.

2. **The same model name appears across different brands.** `Sherman` = La-Z-Boy **and** Franklin. `Aspen` = La-Z-Boy sectional piece **and** Franklin recliner. `Chandler` = Franklin (the brief listed it under Flexsteel). `Kent` = Ashley **and** Franklin. `Top Tier` appears in both the Ashley and Franklin listings at the same $699.99. **Never key a SKU on model name alone.**

3. **Slumberland and Homemakers disagree by 20–50% on the same chair.** Randell fabric: $1,149.99 (SLBD) vs $777.99–$778.99 (HMKR). Joshua: $699.99 (SLBD) vs not carried. Morrison: $669.99 (SLBD) vs $497.99 (HMKR). Astor: $999.99 (SLBD) vs $698.99 (HMKR). Trouper: $779.99 (SLBD) vs $535.00 (HMKR). **These are different retailers with different cost bases, different cover grades under the same model name, and different promo cadences — not an error in either.** For assortment work, Slumberland's own numbers are the relevant street price; Homemakers is the competitive check.

4. **Homemakers shows two different prices for the same SKU on two different URLs.** Tulen Gray: $339.99 on the recliners path, $429.00 on the root path. Ernestine Truffle: $659.99 vs $719.00. Warnerton: $829.99 vs $939.00. The recliners-category path appears to carry the promotional price and the root path the regular price. **We recorded both. Prefer the lower/category-path figure as street.**

5. **All "was" prices are retailer comparison prices, not MSRP.** Discounts run 17%–64% and several exceed 50% (Draycoll 64%, Vail 55%, Kodiak 52%, Paddington Power 53%). Franklin's lift chairs show the most extreme spreads (Clayton Gold $2,199.99 against a "was" of $5,162.06 — a 57% claim). **Do not treat any "was" figure as a manufacturer price or use it to infer margin.**

6. **The entire Slumberland catalogue currently reads "Unavailable for purchase."** Every product row across all collection pages carried this status on the pull date. Prices are published and current, but **live inventory could not be confirmed for a single SKU.** This may be an online-ordering restriction rather than an out-of-stock condition, but it means we cannot distinguish active from discontinued models on that site.

7. **Mechanism for most rows is inferred from the product name, not read off a spec sheet.** The naming convention is consistent and was spot-checked, but two La-Z-Boy terms are **inferred and not confirmed by a vendor source**: we read **"Power Plus" = power recline + power headrest** and **"Tri-Power" = power recline + headrest + lumbar**. The supporting evidence is the La-Z-Boy SKU prefix pattern seen in search results (`10U502` = "Power Rocking Recliner w/ Headrest"; `10X502` / `10X777` = "w/ Headrest & Lumbar") plus the consistent price laddering. **Confirm with the vendor before writing these into spec sheets.**

8. **"Special Purchase" is a real La-Z-Boy/Slumberland construct, but its price level is not what the brief says.** Slumberland's Joshua page indexes with the title *"Joshua Special Purchase Rocker Recliner"*, and a *"Randell Special Purchase Rocker Recliner Chair"* in Silt Brown surfaced in search. So the program exists. **But both chairs price at $699.99 and $1,149.99 respectively, not $299.** Either the $299 figure is stale, is a different program, is an event-only doorbuster not currently live, or is wrong. **Do not build a $299 opening price point on it.** The lowest verified La-Z-Boy is Collage at $399.99 and the lowest verified recliner of any governed brand is Ashley Nerviano at $179.99 (Homemakers) / $269.99 (Slumberland).
